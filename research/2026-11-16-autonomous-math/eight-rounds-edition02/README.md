# Eight Rounds — MathWise | Publication Edition 02

**A documented experiment in autonomous mathematical research**  
Grounded DI LLC · MathWise · Protocol A · November 16, 2026

Eight substantial mathematical investigations were conducted in one ChatGPT FastPath Pro thread using code-assisted exact checks, proof attempts, counterexample searches, critical review, and retained artifacts. The rounds were **manually authorized**, not unattended hourly executions.

This is a **research book**, not a claim to have solved eight previously open problems. The publication records what the system tried, what it actually established, what failed, and the exact boundaries of verification.

## Research outcomes

| Round | Subject | Recorded result |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Erdős–Ginzburg–Ziv | **Candidate proof** — reconstruction of an established theorem |
| 02 | Quadratic abundance of zero-sum selections | **Counterexample found** — at modulus 4, seven qualifying subsets versus a proposed bound of nine |
| 03 | Weighted matrix-tree theorem and negative dependence | **Candidate proof** — reconstruction of established mathematics |
| 04 | Spanning-tree covariance after adding an edge | **Counterexample found** — the monotonicity conjecture fails |
| 05 | Matching-polynomial real-rootedness | **Candidate proof** — reconstruction of established mathematics |
| 06 | Matching-size response to edge addition | **Counterexample found** — a weighted six-vertex witness and a unit-weight family |
| 07 | Galvin's bipartite list edge-coloring theorem | **Candidate proof** — reconstruction of an established theorem |
| 08 | Arbitrary equal-sized edge-list coloring counts | **Partial** — restricted proofs and finite search, no universal resolution |

**Distribution:** 4 candidate theorem reconstructions; 3 exact counterexamples to conjectures formulated for these experiments; 1 unresolved universal investigation.
